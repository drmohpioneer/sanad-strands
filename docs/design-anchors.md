# Design anchors (the owner's own words; read before any design or content work on a surface)

Recorded 2026-09-12 from the owner's review of the three demo pages and his pick among three directions. These are anchors, not suggestions: a brief that contradicts them is wrong before it is measured.

## Chosen direction: Aurora Deck (2026-09-12)
Deep navy ground, glass layers, indigo to cyan glow, real motion on load and interaction, dark first with a designed light variant. The reference mockup lives in the private lane folder (`lane/design-directions-2026-09-12/direction-1/index.html`); its tokens, recipes and motion are copied into the look contract. The other two directions (Ember Room, Signal Black) were also liked; Signal Black's slide-in patient panel may be borrowed.

## Feel
- Premium, cinematic, with depth: gradients, layered surfaces, glow, motion that feels alive. Interactive, not static.
- Colour used with intent. Not a rainbow, not one grey. "So clean it is blunt" is a failure.
- Dark first. Light is a second designed look, not a derivative. No "system" option that looks like light.
- Never modelled on medical websites ("they are boring"). Premium consumer and pro tools are the references.

## Reading
- Every page speaks the doctor's or the patient's language, never the engine's state names ("Needs review", "Material change since the original review", "Awaiting link", "coverage" are failures).
- Each patient row says in one sentence what to do and why; on the patients list a click opens the patient's card inside the list, and "Open the full record" opens the profile (owner, 2026-09-13 22:55).
- English only on the doctor surface (his decision); Arabic on the patient side delivers meaning the way an Egyptian doctor would say it.
- Human to human: if a sentence would not be said across a desk, rewrite it.

## Process
- Options first: two or three distinct directions as browser mockups with motion; the owner picks before a line of product code.
- Content and look are separate contracts; content runs first.
- Measured checks (contrast, layout, motion gates) verify a direction he chose; they never choose for him.
